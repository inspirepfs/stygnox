# Contributing to StygNox — Licensing and Credit

This file is intended to complement the repository's normal development and contribution guidance.

## Contribution licence

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions accepted into StygNox are incorporated into the community edition under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

To preserve StygNox's dual-licensing model, substantive external contributions also require acceptance of [`CLA.md`](CLA.md).

## Pull-request sign-off

Include the following confirmation in each substantive external pull request:

```text
CLA: I have read and agree to the StygNox Contributor Licence Agreement
version 1.0 for this Contribution, and I confirm that I have the right
to submit it.
```

A maintainer should record the acceptance before merging.

For trivial corrections where copyright significance is negligible, a maintainer may decide that separate CLA recording is unnecessary. When in doubt, record the CLA acceptance.

## Contributions made for an employer

If the contribution was created within the scope of employment or another organisation may own the relevant IP, the contributor must confirm that they have authority to submit it. Maintainers may ask for organisational confirmation for significant contributions.

## Third-party code

Do not submit code, documentation, generated assets or other material copied from a third party unless its origin and licence are clearly identified and are compatible with StygNox's licensing model.

## AI-assisted contributions

If generative tooling materially contributed to a submission, the human contributor remains responsible for verifying that the submitted material can lawfully be contributed and does not knowingly reproduce incompatible third-party material. Record material AI assistance when the repository's contribution process requests it.

## Recognition

StygNox aims to credit meaningful work. For significant features, include provenance in the relevant issue, Idea/Feature Registry item or pull request so that `CREDITS.md`, `CONTRIBUTORS.md` and release notes can identify the appropriate originators, designers, implementers, reviewers and validators.

See [`RECOGNITION.md`](RECOGNITION.md).
