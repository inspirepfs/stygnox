# StygNox Licensing

StygNox uses a dual-licensing model designed to keep the community edition genuinely open source while preserving a path for proprietary and embedded commercial use.

## Community licence

Unless a file states otherwise, StygNox source code in this repository is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License version 3 only** (`AGPL-3.0-only`).

The complete licence text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

Commercial use is permitted under the AGPL. No fee, royalty, sponsorship or donation is required to exercise rights granted by the AGPL, provided its terms are followed.

For modified versions that users interact with remotely over a computer network, pay particular attention to section 13 of the AGPL and the requirement to offer those users access to the Corresponding Source.

## Alternative commercial licensing

The StygNox copyright holders may also offer StygNox under separate commercial terms. This can be useful where an organisation wants rights that are not compatible with the AGPL, for example proprietary redistribution, a closed-source derivative, or embedding StygNox into a product under terms that cannot satisfy the AGPL.

See [`COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md`](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md).

## Contributions

Contributors retain copyright in their own contributions. To preserve the project's ability to offer both the AGPL community edition and alternative commercial licences, substantive contributions are accepted under the StygNox Contributor Licence Agreement in [`CLA.md`](CLA.md).

The CLA does **not** transfer ownership of a contribution to the project. It grants the project the additional rights needed to distribute and relicense accepted contributions while the contributor keeps their copyright.

See [`CONTRIBUTING-LICENSING.md`](CONTRIBUTING-LICENSING.md) for the contribution sign-off process.

## Recognition

StygNox intends to recognise both implementation work and the people who originate material ideas, designs and improvements. Recognition is governed by [`RECOGNITION.md`](RECOGNITION.md), with durable records in [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md) and [`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md).

Recognition is a project commitment and community practice; it does not change the rights granted by the AGPL or CLA.

## SPDX identifier

New StygNox source files should normally include:

```text
SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-only
```

Where a file is subject to different terms, state those terms explicitly in that file.

## Important

This repository material is intended to document the StygNox project's licensing model. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Before entering into a material commercial licensing agreement, or if ownership/employment/IP issues could affect the code, obtain appropriate legal review.
