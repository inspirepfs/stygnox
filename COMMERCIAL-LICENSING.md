StygNox is available to the community under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`). The AGPL permits commercial use when its terms are followed.

The StygNox copyright holders may also make the software available under a separate commercial licence.

## When a commercial licence may be useful

A separate commercial licence may be appropriate where an organisation wants to:

- distribute StygNox or a derivative under proprietary terms;
- operate a modified StygNox service without meeting applicable AGPL source-availability obligations;
- embed or combine StygNox with a product where the intended licensing model is incompatible with the AGPL;
- obtain separately negotiated support, warranty, indemnity, service levels or other contractual terms.

A commercial licence is an alternative grant from the relevant copyright holders. It does not remove or reduce rights already granted to users who choose to comply with the AGPL.

## Community and commercial users

StygNox is intended to be useful to individuals, open-source communities and businesses alike. Organisations using StygNox under the AGPL do not owe a licence fee, royalty or donation merely because their use is commercial.

If StygNox creates meaningful value for you or your organisation, financial sponsorship, engineering contributions, documentation, testing and other forms of support are warmly encouraged. Such support is voluntary and is not a condition of the AGPL licence.

## Small-scale support

The project deliberately leaves room for lightweight support — including the equivalent of "buy me a coffee" — as well as larger sponsorship arrangements. A funding link can be published in the repository README or GitHub funding configuration when the project owner chooses a provider.

## Enquiries

For alternative commercial licensing, use the licensing contact method published by the StygNox repository owner or open a repository discussion/issue specifically marked as a licensing enquiry.

Do not send confidential commercial information in a public issue.
